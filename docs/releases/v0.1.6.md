## HeadOrbit 0.1.6 (12) — experimental fork / 实验性改进分支

Based on Cogria-AI/HeadOrbit v0.1.5; MIT license and original attribution retained.

### Features and improvements / 功能与改进

- Automatic reference recovery after reconnecting, triggered by one click, key, or scroll, with tolerance for small natural head movements. / 重连后一次操作即可启动恢复，允许自然小幅头部动作。
- Full relative Yaw/Pitch/Roll calibration and restored posture reminder activation after reference recovery. / 完整三轴相对校准，参考恢复后接通坐姿提醒。
- Bluetooth input/output route observation, bounded retries, clearer connection and recovery status. / 蓝牙输入输出变化检测、有上限的重试及更明确的状态提示。
- `⌃⌥⌘C` manual recentering, explicit diagnostic CSV export, and complete version/build display. / 快捷键手动归零、主动导出诊断及完整版本显示。

- Build 12 preserves the reference across brief callback delays, pauses effects while data is stale, and excludes backlog from calibration. / 构建 12 在短暂回调延迟后保留原参考，旧数据期间暂停效果且不参与校准。

### Validation / 验证

49/49 local replay scenarios passed and were independently repeated, including 48 small-motion combinations. Build 12 live logs confirmed a brief delivery pause followed by resumption with the same reference, and reference invalidation after an actual disconnect. Historical build 11 evidence: one AirPods Pro session verified live Pitch, automatic reference recovery, and posture reminder activation/release. See [validation scope](https://github.com/jimzzzjones/HeadOrbit/blob/v0.1.6/docs/VALIDATION.md).

本地 49/49 个回放场景通过，独立复测通过，包含 48 组小幅动作组合。历史构建 11 的一次 AirPods Pro 实机过程验证了 Pitch、自动参考及坐姿提醒的触发和解除。

### Download and limitations / 下载与限制

Check `ARCHITECTURES.txt` for the packaged executable's architecture and `SHA256SUMS.txt` for the DMG checksum. Builds are ad-hoc signed, **not Apple-notarized**. Downloaded apps may require approval through macOS Privacy & Security after you verify their origin.

Automatic reference is an estimate from the current stable pose. It does not verify upright posture or screen direction, and it does not continuously correct drift. Recovery after every device switch is not guaranteed. Manual recentering remains available. Original author and MIT license are included in the app and DMG.

请检查架构文件和 DMG 校验值。构建使用临时签名，**未经过 Apple 公证**。自动参考只代表当前稳定姿态，不能确认坐直、屏幕方向或持续修正漂移，也不保证每次跨设备重连成功。可随时手动归零。
